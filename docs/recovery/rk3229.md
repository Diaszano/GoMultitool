# RK3229 recovery procedure

The existing recovery card and original boot components remain the recovery route until a Go-based image has passed the [hardware smoke test](../../tests/hardware/rk3229-smoke-test.md). Keep a checksum-verified copy of the known-working card before testing and do not overwrite it.

If a test image fails to boot, remove power, restore the original card, and boot the board with that card. Do not attempt bootloader or NAND repair as part of this procedure. Those operations need a board-specific recovery plan and separate authorization.

No known-working card, physical board, or disposable restore target was available during this implementation. The Go recovery image therefore remains experimental and physical recovery is unverified.
